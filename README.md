## AI

### Why choose not to use AI?
I did for a while, then I took a step back and though to myself "what am I actually gaining from this?". It worked everything out for me, I didn't have to crack any problems or design how something would work. That didn't sit right with me. Now I purposely avoid it, especially for learning purposes. I admit it has it's place in the world but I'm a firm believer learning should be done the hard way, fixing bugs, getting stuck, trawling through a rabbit hole of Stack Overflow posts to find the answer, or simply just work it out myself. That's what's fun to me and will be beneficial for the rest of my software development career.

If you find AI helpful when you learn, more power to you.
